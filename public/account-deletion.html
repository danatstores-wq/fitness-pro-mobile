Request Account Deletion

FITNESS PRO BY DANAT

If you created a Fitness Pro account and want it deleted, you can submit a request without reinstalling or signing in to the app. If you are signed in, you can also submit a deletion request directly from Profile → Privacy & Account Data inside the app.

How to request deletion

  1. For requests made outside the app, send an email to IT@danat-gulf.com from the email address associated with your Fitness Pro account, where possible.
  2. Use the subject line Fitness Pro account deletion request.
  3. Include the email address used for your Fitness Pro account. Do not include your password.
  4. We may contact you to verify account ownership before processing the request.
Email account deletion request

What will be deleted

After verifying and processing your request, DANAT will delete the associated Fitness Pro Firebase Authentication account and the server-side terms-acceptance record, together with the deletion-request record and any other server-side account information held for the account, except information that must be retained for a legitimate legal or security reason. If any information must be retained, we will explain the applicable reason. DANAT will send confirmation to the account email address when processing is complete.

Local device data: Your profile, food diary, body measurements, goals, meal plans, reminders, activity logs and profile photo are stored locally on your device in the current app; they are not uploaded to the Fitness Pro Firebase project. An account-deletion request cannot remotely erase data stored only on your phone. To remove those local records, clear Fitness Pro's app storage in your device settings or uninstall the app.

Questions

Contact IT@danat-gulf.com. See the Fitness Pro Privacy Policy for more information about data handling.
