2. Information handled by the app

  • Account information: when you register, Firebase Authentication processes your email address, account identifier and authentication information. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; the app does not store your password in its local fitness database.
  • Terms acceptance record: the app stores your Firebase user identifier, accepted terms version, effective date, acceptance method and acceptance timestamp in Firebase Firestore.
  • Account deletion requests: if you submit an in-app account deletion request, Firebase Firestore stores your user identifier, account email address, request timestamp and pending status so DANAT can process the request. The request record is removed after the account deletion is completed.
  • Local fitness information: profile details, age, sex, height, weight, body measurements, goals, food diary entries, custom meals, meal plans, reminders, activity logs, challenge check-ins, language settings and profile photo are stored in the app's local SQLite database on your device.
  • Step count: if you choose to connect a supported health platform and grant permission, the app reads today's step count from Apple HealthKit or Android Health Connect. The current implementation requests read access to step count only; it does not write health data to those services or upload step counts to the Fitness Pro Firebase project. Step data is used only to display your activity progress. It is not shared with Google AdMob or other advertising partners, used for advertising targeting or measurement, or sold.
  • Camera and barcode: the camera is used to scan a product barcode. The barcode number is sent to supported Open Food Facts family product databases to look up product and nutrition information. The scanner does not upload a photo or video to DANAT as part of the barcode lookup.
  • Advertising and consent: the app uses Google AdMob for banner and interstitial advertising and Google's consent-management tools. Depending on your region, consent choices and device settings, Google may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, app and device information, diagnostics, consent status and ad interaction information under Google's policies.
  • Reminders: meal and water reminders are scheduled as local device notifications. The app does not use remote push notifications in its current configuration.

3. How information is used and shared

Information is used to provide the app's features, authenticate accounts, record terms acceptance, process account-deletion requests, retrieve barcode product information, display advertisements where permitted, schedule reminders and troubleshoot technical problems.

  • Google Firebase: processes account authentication and stores the terms-acceptance and account-deletion request records described above.
  • Google AdMob: provides banner and interstitial ads and processes information as described in Google's privacy documentation and your consent/device settings.
  • Open Food Facts family services: receive the scanned barcode to return product information. These services operate independently and their own privacy terms apply.
  • Apple HealthKit / Android Health Connect: provide step-count data only after you grant permission. The app reads the daily step count and does not write data back.

The current app implementation does not send your locally stored food diary, profile, measurements, goals, meal plans, reminders or activity logs to DANAT's Firebase project. DANAT does not receive those local records through the app's current Firebase configuration.

4. Storage and security

Local fitness records are kept in the app's private storage on your device. Protect your device with an operating-system passcode or other device security controls. Account authentication and the terms-acceptance record are handled by Firebase over encrypted network connections. Third-party providers are responsible for the security and processing of information they receive under their own terms and policies. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Retention and deletion

Local fitness information is stored on your device until you clear the app's storage, uninstall the app, or submit an in-app account-deletion request. The in-app deletion flow attempts to erase the local profile, food diary, measurements, goals, meal plans, reminders, activity logs, profile photo, user-entered foods and scanned-food cache, and cancels scheduled mobile reminders. The bundled food catalogue and app translations are retained so the app can still open as a guest.

If you submit a deletion request from outside the app, DANAT cannot remotely erase records stored only on your device. If the in-app local erasure cannot complete, clear Fitness Pro's app storage in your device settings or uninstall the app. Device and operating-system backups may have separate retention behavior.

You may request deletion of your Fitness Pro account and server-side account records by following the instructions at Account Deletion or emailing IT@danat-gulf.com. We may ask you to verify that you control the account. Some limited information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes; any such retention will be explained when applicable.
